Decisions and open questions

These became team decisions. The questions still open are listed with who answers them. The button copies the open list.

Decided

  1. Placement:in front of the messaging gateway, receiving every provider callback.
  2. Weight changes:never move a customer in progress. After 100 percent, v1 conversation history moves into the v2 database.
  3. Data:kept in one region, accepted. The phone number is stored only as an HMAC, and the record ends 30 days after the last message or at a sale, then the store deletes it.
  4. Hosting:router on AWS; the v2 assistant stays on its container platform. Up to 300 ms of added latency is acceptable.
  5. Follow-ups:do not ask the router. A customer keeps its version for 30 days after its last message.
  6. Observability:tracing is required, exported to the tracing backend used in v1.
  7. Owner in production:Sam.
  8. Customer keeps its version:30 days after its last message or until a sale.
  9. Silence handoff job:asks the router who owns the customer before sending it to the queue.
  10. Stages:5, 10, 25, 50 and 100 percent, after a pass-through stage at 0 percent.
  11. Stage exit criteria:minimum conversations and margins on handoff, errors and sales, set by the business owner.
  12. Emergency rollback:accepted when v1 reads what v2 collected, and never for customers with an agent or in closing.

  4. Who reports a sale to end the customer's assignment, and from which source?Pat and Sam. Sales records arrive late.Q28

  6. Should router telemetry also go to the service catalog?Dana with Fred.Q27

  8. Where does session_facts live, and can both versions read and write it by phone?Sam. The emergency rollback depends on it.Q34
  9. Which synthetic event can a stage entry probe send without touching a real customer?Sam and the platform team.Q35
  10. Does compliance need a maximum delay between the end of an assignment and its deletion?The product owner, with legal. Today deletion follows the store's expiry, typically within a few days.Q36
