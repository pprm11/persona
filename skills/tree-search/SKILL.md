# Tree Search

Set a resource ceiling and define the decision or questions to answer. This workflow grants no new authorization for delegation or external changes.

1. Search the initial question, open promising sources, and record what each establishes and what remains uncertain.
2. Expand from the evidence, gaps and contradictions: derive consequential child questions and search terms, retaining their links to the parent. Prune duplicates and irrelevant branches instead of following a fixed keyword checklist.
3. Compare sources, favor primary evidence, check date and context, and investigate plausible alternatives when an identity or premise is uncertain. Separate observation, inference and unknowns.
4. Prioritize unresolved questions that could change the conclusion. Stop when the task is answered and two relevant rounds add no meaningful distinction or evidence, or when the declared ceiling is reached. Saturation is not proof of exhaustive knowledge.

At the ceiling report consequential gaps without claiming saturation. Offer an extension only when needed to meet the goal. Deliver a sourced synthesis and, if useful, a compact search tree or ledger.
