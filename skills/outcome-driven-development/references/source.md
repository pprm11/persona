# Source and adaptation

Primary source: [Danial Hasan, The Software Factory Trap](https://x.com/danialhasan/status/2057519809017897061).
The source argues for preserving intent, decision context, source authority and evidence around
generated code, while accounting for the cost of maintaining that machinery. It distinguishes
proof of a technical part from proof of the promised product outcome.

This skill operationalizes those ideas. Its detailed protocols, examples and adoption/upgrade
guidance are original adaptations for the user's software work, not quoted instructions or a
claim that the article specifies this exact implementation. Guidance about new and legacy projects and framework propagation extends those ideas for the user's requirements.
