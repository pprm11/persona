# Coordination and review without reconstruction

## Choose the mode by the remaining judgment

Proceed autonomously where requirements, source authority, permissions and proof surfaces are
sufficient. Collaborate on decisions involving material product taste, clarity, trust, changed
business promises or conflicting authority. A small mechanical UI edit may be well specified;
a backend migration can require a product/owner decision. File category is not permission.

Ask a focused question only when that decision cannot be resolved from the available evidence.
Explain the concrete choice and continue independent work. Do not demand approval of a vague
plan when the authorized work can first produce a concrete reviewable candidate.

## Shared boundaries before parallel execution

When delegation is actually authorized and useful, assign distinct hypotheses or owned scopes.
Give each worker the same target, contract references and relevant authority. Establish one writer
for shared policy/abstractions, a stopping condition and a bounded resource envelope. Repeating the
same full suite or the same review persona does not create independence.

If two work streams disagree, surface the conflicting assumptions, sources, affected contract
and proposed resolution. Reconcile routine implementation conflicts within authorization; escalate
only the material unresolved judgment. Messages from agents do not grant human permissions.
Preserve other work and ownership when scope changes or is revoked.

## Review packet

Present the smallest packet that lets the reviewer decide without replaying the session:

1. The original user/problem outcome and the delivered slice.
2. What changed, the material choice and the source that justifies it.
3. Claims linked to executed evidence, target/version and known limit.
4. Failed attempts, remaining uncertainty or stale inputs relevant to the decision.
5. Adoption/operation/maintenance consequence and the actual pending decision, if any.

A compact table plus artifact links often suffices. Do not dump all traces, create a new summary
system or use document length as rigor. Label code present, test executed, effect observed and
product accepted separately. Capture a material review decision when it occurs, including what
it supersedes and whether prevention was actually verified. A finding remains useful before a
new gate exists.

Completion is comparison against the original intent. If the spec drifted away from the user
problem, passing the spec's tests does not finish the work. Automated metrics can inform a
developer-experience review; they do not invent the user's assessment of clarity or confidence.
