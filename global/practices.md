# Practices (persona level)

Version: v0.1. The working practices of one person, published on purpose and opt-in: how they plan, review,
write and delegate. Rules that are valid for anyone belong in `superego`; rules of a team in `collective`; one
company's terms in `ego`; private history and settings in `id`.

## How to use

- Keep each practice to a few lines, with the reason it exists.
- Put long procedures in a skill under `skills/`, with `level: persona` in its frontmatter.
- Everything here is English and carries no private data, because the layer is meant to be opened.
